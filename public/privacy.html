Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

ShiftDrop Pro ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by PixelNotch Studio, is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and manage information across our mobile app and web platform.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account Details: Email address and profile name provided via email registration or Google OAuth.
  • Courier Telemetry: Optional mileage, fuel rates, shift timestamps, and parcel manifest logs recorded to calculate courier tax shield write-offs.
  • Device Hardware: Biometric authentication (stored exclusively on your local hardware device; never uploaded to external servers).

2. Data Security & Storage

Your session and telemetry data are encrypted in transit and stored in protected Supabase database clusters governed by Row Level Security (RLS). We never sell your personal information or route telemetry.

3. Account Deletion & Queries

You may request account or telemetry data deletion at any time by contacting developer support at joanne@pixelnotchstudio.com.
